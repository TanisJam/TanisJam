### Experiments

**[LIME](https://github.com/TanisJam/lime)** — Live Interactive Music Engine
You don't tell it which notes to play; you tell it how the music should *feel*. It composes
a few bars ahead, remembering and developing material as the mood shifts. No loops, no
crossfades. **[▶ Play with it](https://dist-pi-indol-64.vercel.app)**

**[Lifelines](https://github.com/TanisJam/lifelines)** — a town you can fork
A Dwarf Fortress–style life simulator. Every choice in every life is a recorded decision —
click one, pick a different option, and watch the town's history re-simulate and diverge
from the original. Simulate first, then describe: every sentence comes from the event log.

**[Citrus × sinensis](https://github.com/TanisJam/citrus-sinensis)** — the front door of mnr.ar
The life cycle of an orange tree, seed to seed, drawn on a 2D canvas.
